The project, briefly.

As a Multirotors Engineering Intern, I investigated, debugged, and documented an existing UAV platform’s flight-control, companion-computer, communications, telemetry, and video interfaces. I contributed configuration documentation and participated in system-integration and thrust testing; aircraft development remained a wider team effort.

I also developed a Python/Tkinter tool to estimate flight time and range from aircraft, payload, mission, and environmental inputs. Together, these workstreams connected hands-on integration with engineering documentation and performance estimation.
