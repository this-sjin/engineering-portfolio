Overview

During my internship at Heven Aerotech, I worked on integration and troubleshooting across an existing UAV’s flight controller, companion computer, and communications systems. I documented subsystem interfaces and configurations, supported telemetry and video setup, and participated in integration and thrust testing with the engineering team.

I also developed a Python/Tkinter tool to estimate flight time and range from aircraft, payload, mission, and environmental inputs. Working on both integration and performance modeling gave me a broader understanding of the complete aircraft.
