Modular quadcopter

A custom frame connecting mechanical design with flight-control setup

  • Mechanical design
  • Prototyping
  • UAV systems

The project, briefly.

I designed a modular quadcopter frame intended for repairable construction and GPS-guided missions. The mixed-material assembly combines FDM-printed plates, clamps, and mounts with tube arms.

I integrated the flight controller, GPS, and power distribution, then configured PX4 and carried out flight tuning. The project connected custom mechanical interfaces and electronics packaging to a built aircraft; the historical portfolio also reports waypoint and automatic-flight trials.
